❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
• Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
- AgroPulse is a unified digital platform enabling Scheduled Time-Slot Booking, Dynamic QR Token E-Passes, and Real-Time Mandi Queue Tracking.
- AI Fair Price & Quality Valuation Engine analyzing APMC trends, Government MSP floors, and moisture adjustments to protect farmer margins.
- Smart Kisan Freight Pooling for consolidated rural milk-run transport, cutting farmgate transit costs by up to 60%.
• How it addresses the problem (SIH26032)
- Eliminates Long Waiting Times: Replaces chaotic unannounced arrivals with slotted hourly arrival windows, cutting wait times from 12+ hrs to <30 mins.
- Delivers 100% Schedule Clarity: Instant SMS, WhatsApp, and portal notifications providing exact counter, slot date, and arrival timing.
- Removes Procurement Uncertainty: Live step-by-step tracking (Listing → In-Transit → Checked-in → Inspection → Instant DBT Payout Voucher).
• Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
- Multi-Stage Cryptographic QR Token: Single verifiable pass connecting farmgate booking, weighbridge inspection, and Direct Bank DBT payout.
- Proportional Freight Sharing Algorithm: Dynamic fare allocation based on farmer crop weight and pickup corridor distance.
- Offline-Tolerant Gate Validation: Encrypted QR tokens validated locally at mandi gate scanners during rural internet outages.

• Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
- Technical Feasibility: High — Lightweight RESTful microservice architecture tested with rapid response times (<100ms) and low bandwidth overhead.
- Operational Feasibility: Smooth onboarding with minimal learning curve; mandi inspectors use intuitive web scan consoles without modifying existing weigh scales.
- Economic Viability: Highly viable with near-zero CAPEX, self-sustainable through nominal transaction audit fees and logistics optimization savings.
• Potential challenges and risks
- Intermittent Internet Connectivity: Potential connectivity drops in rural mandi locations during peak procurement season.
- Farmer Digital Literacy: Hesitancy among elder or smallholder farmers with mobile application interfaces.
- Surge Arrival Peaks: Vehicles arriving outside their booked time windows causing temporary queue variations.
• Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Offline Cryptographic Validation: QR passes store self-contained digital signatures, allowing gate verification without active internet connection.
- Multilingual Voice & SMS IVR: Automated slot updates, arrival reminders, and payment receipts dispatched via regional SMS and WhatsApp.
- Dynamic Buffer Management: Intelligent 30-minute grace arrival periods and automated counter workload re-routing.
